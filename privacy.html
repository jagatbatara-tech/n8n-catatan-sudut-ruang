Privacy Policy

Catatan Sudut Ruang

Effective Date: August 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Catatan Sudut Ruang content management and publishing application processes information when connecting to authorized social media accounts.

1. Information We Process

The application may process basic account information required for authentication, such as an account identifier, display name, profile image, and authorization credentials.

The application may also process video files, thumbnail images, captions, hashtags, metadata, and publishing settings selected by an authorized user.

2. Purpose of Processing

Information is processed to authenticate authorized accounts, prepare content, prevent duplicate uploads, and upload or publish selected content through official platform APIs.

3. TikTok Data

When an authorized user connects a TikTok account, the application may receive account information and authorization tokens made available through TikTok APIs.

TikTok data is used only to provide the account connection and content publishing functions requested by the authorized user.

4. Local Files

Videos, thumbnail images, metadata files, and workflow records may be stored locally on the device running the application.

Local files may be archived or deleted after successful processing, according to the workflow configuration selected by the user.

5. Data Sharing

The application sends selected content and required authorization information to connected platforms through their official APIs.

Personal information is not sold, rented, or shared with unrelated third parties for advertising purposes.

6. Data Retention

Account authorization data may be retained while the connected account remains authorized. Upload records may be retained to prevent duplicate publications and diagnose failed operations.

Local files and records may be deleted when they are no longer required for application operation.

7. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect credentials, access tokens, locally stored files, and workflow information from unauthorized access.

8. User Control and Revocation

Users may revoke the application's access through the security or application settings of the connected platform.

Users may also request deletion of locally stored content, account connection information, and workflow records under their control.

9. Children's Privacy

The application is not designed for or intentionally directed to children, and the application does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when application features, platform requirements, or applicable regulations change.

11. Contact

Privacy-related questions may be submitted through the contact information associated with Catatan Sudut Ruang.
