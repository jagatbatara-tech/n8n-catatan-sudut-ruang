About This Application

Catatan Sudut Ruang is a content management and publishing application designed to help authorized creators prepare and publish original short-form video content.

The application processes locally prepared videos and metadata, then connects to authorized social media accounts through official platform APIs.

Access to account information and publishing features requires explicit authorization from the account owner.
